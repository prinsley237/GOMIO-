How GOMIO Works for You

Explore the tools that make ordering, growing a food business, and delivering with GOMIO simpler.

For Customers

Easy Restaurant Discovery

Find restaurants near you and narrow your choices by cuisine, rating, delivery time, and price. Clear information helps you choose with confidence.

Secure Mobile Payments

Pay conveniently through MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money. Your order is confirmed without needing to handle cash at delivery.

Real-Time Tracking

Follow your order as it moves from the restaurant to your door, so you know when it is being prepared and when it is close.

Fast Delivery

GOMIO connects your order with nearby restaurants and riders to help deliver fresh meals quickly and reliably.

Ratings & Reviews

Use customer feedback to choose your next meal, then share your own experience to help the GOMIO community.

Favorites & Reordering

Keep your favorite restaurants and meals close at hand, making repeat orders fast when you already know what you want.

For Restaurants

Restaurant Dashboard

Manage menus, review incoming orders, and keep your restaurant information up to date from one practical workspace.

Reach New Customers

Put your menu in front of hungry customers nearby and grow beyond the people who already know your location.

Analytics & Insights

Understand order patterns, customer preferences, and performance so you can make sharper decisions about your menu and service.

24/7 Support

Get help with orders, platform questions, and operational issues from the GOMIO support team whenever you need it.

Marketing Tools

Highlight special offers and promote popular meals to attract attention and encourage more customers to place an order.

For Delivery Riders

Smart Route Optimization

Use clear delivery information and route guidance to reduce unnecessary travel and complete more deliveries efficiently.

Flexible Earnings

Choose a schedule that works for you and keep track of delivery activity and earnings as you work.

Insurance Coverage

Delivery support and coverage help riders work with greater peace of mind while serving GOMIO customers.

Performance Bonuses

Strong ratings, dependable service, and consistent delivery performance can open the door to additional rewards.

Rider App

Access order details, navigation, and communication tools from a rider-focused mobile experience.

Community Support

Connect with fellow riders, exchange practical advice, and get support as you build your delivery work with GOMIO.
