Solving Food Delivery Challenges in Cameroon & Africa

GOMIO was born from the need to solve real food delivery problems faced by millions across Cameroon and Africa. We understand the challenges of accessing quality food quickly and affordably.

Our mission is to connect hungry customers with great local restaurants while providing fair opportunities for delivery riders. We're building a food delivery ecosystem that works for everyone - customers, restaurants, and riders.
